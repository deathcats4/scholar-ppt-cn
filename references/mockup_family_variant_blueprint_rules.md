# Mockup Family and Variant Rules

Create a family blueprint only when it improves reusable visual planning.

Useful cases include:

- long decks;
- repeated evidence types;
- several related result pages;
- explicit sample-first requests;
- expansion from approved visual directions.

Skip it for simple planning, one-off diagrams, or local slide revisions when it
adds no value. Record the reason in `workflow.steps`.

When used, record:

- family ID, name, purpose, applicable asset geometries, and slide IDs;
- variant ID, family ID, name, structure, and slide IDs;
- the selected family/variant and decision reason on each mapped slide;
- representative sample slide IDs when visual samples are planned.

Define enough variants to avoid repetition; do not enforce a fixed minimum when
the deck does not need it. Families guide composition and are not rigid
stencils.
